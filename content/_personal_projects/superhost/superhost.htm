SUPERHOST (2025)

Unreal Engine 5.6

For the Epic Megajam 2025 - with the theme "Here We Go Again" - I had started out with an initial idea about our daily struggle of work & life balance. Although the idea worked for me, I realized quickly that I wouldn't be able to finish it in time before the game jam's one week deadline. I needed to pivot to something a little bit more manageable; what if you were a Bed & Breakfast owner, with new guests arriving?
I love cozy, cleaning and restoration games, so cleaning after messy guests would be a fun topic to explore. Your guests leave a star rating and review based on the cleanliness and effort of keeping your place in order. And this became SuperHost. After the Megajam, I released an update with more stability, scenarios, fixes and overall quality of life improvements.

 

My work on "SuperHost" included:

  • Game framework and flow (restart, loading screen, flows)
  • Created the UI & UX flow of the menus  
  • Cleaning mechanics: return to spot, dispose and wipe surface    
  • Player and camera controls
  • Asset integration and material creation
  • Full prop- and set dressing
  • Full lighting pass using Lumen
  • Optimizations

Game Description

Search for the mess left behind by your guests to prepare the apartment for the arrival of the next one. Spotless welcome will result in a praise, so clean it well. Better zoom in on the details - missed spot may hurt your rating! 

 

Featuring:

  • Cozy apartment with tilt-shift zoom mechanics
  • Clean after 6 different guests
  • Dispose trash, wipe stains
  • Find objects that don't belong
  • Review system reflects your results
  • Relaxed gameplay, take all the time you need
  • Login/profile creation screen, and progress saving
  • Possibility to restart the game after last level
