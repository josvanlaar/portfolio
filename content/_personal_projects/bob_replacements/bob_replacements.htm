"Replacements" (2026)

Unreal Engine 5.7

One of my all-time favorite TV shows is HBO's "Band of Brothers" (2001). For its 25th year anniversary, I faithfully recreated its fourth episode "Replacements" in Unreal Engine 5.7.

My work on "Replacements" included:

  • Recreating the environment of Nuenen, closely based on the TV show
  • Modeling the base geometry, using Unreal Engine 5's Modeling Tools
  • Asset integration and (master) material creation
  • Full prop- and set dressing
  • Full lighting pass using Lumen
  • Character (Metahuman) integration, rigging and animating using Control Rig
  • AI- and character behavior implementations
  • Event creation, scripting, Chaos Fracture, FX implementations
  • Cinematic implementation using Unreal Engine's Level Sequencer

Description

This year marks the 25th anniversary of one of my all-time favorite shows - HBO's "Band of Brothers" - yet it hasn't aged much. 

Each episode is a masterpiece on it's own, but this one has always felt most significant... Maybe because I'm Dutch, so the events depicted are hitting closer to home.

 

 "Replacements" follows the men of Easy Company and the 506th during Operation Market Garden in The Netherlands in September 1944. The operation sadly resulted in many casualties, even though the country was supposed to be 'defended only by German kids and old men'.

18 years ago I recreated Nuenen in a Call of Duty 2 mod - sharing it in my first ever YouTube video. Since then game development became my profession, and Unreal engine - my tool of choice. Feels almost poetic to be paying a tribute by revisiting Nuenen after all of these years - with current skills, knowledge, and technical possibilities. It was still a challenge, but a challenge I needed - I hope I did its justice.
